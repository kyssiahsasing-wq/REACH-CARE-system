About the System

What is REACH-CARE?

Remote Evaluation and Accessible Clinic Healthcare Information System is a proposed digital healthcare platform designed to improve healthcare access in remote communities.

🎯 Our Purpose

To make basic healthcare consultations more accessible to residents in far-flung barangays by connecting them with municipal doctors and supporting local health workers with digital tools.

💡 The Problem We Solve

Long travel distances, expensive transportation, paper-based medical records, and delayed access to patient information can make healthcare difficult. REACH-CARE aims to reduce these barriers through teleconsultation and electronic medical records.

System Features

Healthcare Services and Features

Practical digital tools to support patients and healthcare workers in local communities.

💻

Online Consultations

Enable patients at community health centers to consult municipal doctors through video calls or chat, when available.

📋

Digital Patient Records

Maintain organized electronic medical records, including patient information, symptoms, medical history, and clinical notes.

💊

Digital Prescriptions

Support authorized doctors in preparing electronic prescriptions according to applicable clinical and legal requirements.

🚑

Hospital Referrals

Help healthcare professionals prepare referral notes when patients need further assessment or hospital treatment.

📊

Health Dashboard

Provide authorized health officials with daily and monthly reports to monitor community health trends.

🔐

Secure Access

Organize user accounts and permissions so patients, health workers, doctors, and administrators have appropriate access.

Who Uses the System?

🧑

Patients

Residents of remote communities who need healthcare assistance.

🩺

👨‍⚕️

🖥️

🏛️

Project Requirements

Prioritized System Requirements

The system is designed around essential healthcare functions first, with additional features introduced as resources permit.

🟢 Must-Have

  • Secure user login
  • Electronic medical records
  • Online chat and video consultations
  • Role-based access permissions

🟡 Should-Have

  • Electronic prescriptions
  • Hospital referral management
  • Monthly health reports
  • Health statistics dashboard

⚪ Optional

  • SMS appointment reminders
  • Additional notification features
  • Enhanced reporting tools
System Operations

What the System Needs

Affordable technology and practical operating procedures help make the system suitable for remote communities.

💻

Hardware

Desktop computers, laptops, tablets, or smartphones available at barangay health stations and municipal offices.

🌐

Software and Network

A modern web browser, a secure application server, a database, and internet or mobile data for online services.

🔄

Backups and Training

Tested backup and recovery procedures, security training, and simple instructions for healthcare workers assisting residents.

Privacy, Security, and Trust

A production system must protect patient confidentiality through appropriate permissions, secure data transmission, reliable backups, and compliance with applicable Philippine data privacy and healthcare requirements.
